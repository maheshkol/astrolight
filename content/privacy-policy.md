# Privacy Policy

AstroLight respects your privacy. This page explains the general categories of information that may be processed when you use the website.

## Information entered into calculators

Birth-chart tools may ask for a date, time, geographic coordinates and time-zone information. The current client-side calculation engine is designed to perform the chart calculation in the browser. AstroLight should not require a visitor to create an account merely to calculate a chart.

Do not enter information you do not want processed by a website. Future versions may add optional services such as location search, and this policy will be updated when those services are introduced.

## Analytics and cookies

AstroLight may use privacy-respecting or third-party analytics to understand aggregate traffic, popular pages and general site performance. Such services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

## Advertising

If advertising is enabled, AstroLight may use Google AdSense or other advertising partners. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. The exact providers and settings will be updated here when advertising is enabled.

## External services

Some tools may load third-party libraries or services, such as the astronomical calculation library used by the chart engine. External services have their own privacy policies and terms.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as AstroLight adds analytics, advertising, accounts or external APIs. The current version published on this page is the version that applies to normal site use.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact method provided on the Contact page.
