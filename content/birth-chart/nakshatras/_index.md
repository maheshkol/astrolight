title: "27 Nakshatras"
description: "Explore the 27 Nakshatras, their degree spans, ruling planets and four padas."
---

The sidereal zodiac is divided into 27 Nakshatras of 13°20′ each. Each Nakshatra contains four padas of 3°20′.

AstroLight calculates the Moon's sidereal longitude and maps it into these divisions.
