## Sun ☉

In traditional astrology, **Sun** is used as a symbol for **Identity, Ego, Vitality, Purpose**. AstroLight treats planetary meanings as an interpretive layer placed on top of calculated astronomical positions. The calculation tells us *where* the planet was; astrology supplies a traditional framework for discussing *what that position is said to represent*.

### Core themes

Sun is commonly associated with:

- **Identity**
- **Ego**
- **Vitality**
- **Purpose**

These keywords are deliberately broad. A planet's interpretation changes according to its sign, house, aspects and—within different astrological traditions—the chart system being used.

### Signs and rulership

Traditional rulership tables associate Sun with **Leo**. Rulership is a symbolic relationship, not a physical statement that a planet controls a constellation or human behavior.

When reading a birth chart, the useful question is not simply "What does Sun mean?" but "What sign is Sun in, which house does it occupy, and what other planets form meaningful angular relationships with it?"

### What AstroLight calculates

The chart engine obtains planetary ecliptic longitude from Astronomy Engine. The longitude is expressed as an angle from 0° to 360° along the ecliptic. AstroLight then maps that value into a 30° zodiac sector and calculates house placement from the chosen house model.

For Vedic mode, the tropical longitude is additionally converted into a sidereal longitude using AstroLight's Lahiri-style ayanamsa approximation. The Moon's sidereal longitude is then used to determine its nakshatra and pada.

### Planet in a sign

A planet's **sign** describes the style through which its symbolism is traditionally expressed. For example, the same planet placed in a Fire sign and an Earth sign is interpreted differently because the sign contributes a different element and modality.

### Planet in a house

The **house** describes the life area emphasized by the traditional interpretation. AstroLight's current public calculator uses equal houses, with each house spanning 30° from the calculated Ascendant. This is intentionally disclosed so users understand what the chart engine is doing.

### Planetary aspects

AstroLight also checks angular separation between planetary longitudes. Common aspect families include conjunction, sextile, square, trine and opposition. Each aspect is matched within a configurable orb and reported with its exact angular difference.

## A useful way to read this planet

Start with three questions:

1. **Where is Sun by sign?**
2. **Where is Sun by house?**
3. **Which planets form close aspects to Sun?**

The combination is much more informative than interpreting the planet in isolation.

> **Interpretation note:** These meanings describe traditional astrology. They are not scientifically validated claims about personality, health, relationships or future events.
