description: "Explore the 12 zodiac signs, their 30-degree positions, elements, modalities and ruling planets."
---

The zodiac is a 360° celestial circle traditionally divided into twelve equal signs. Each sign occupies 30° of the circle and is associated with an element, modality and ruling planet.

AstroLight combines this traditional framework with transparent calculations so you can explore not only **what each sign represents**, but also **where it sits on the zodiac circle**.
