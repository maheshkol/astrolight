## Capricorn at a glance

**Capricorn (♑)** is traditionally associated with **Earth**, the **Cardinal** mode, and **Saturn**. The commonly used tropical date range is **December 22 – January 19**. These date ranges are a convenient way to describe Sun-sign astrology; an actual birth chart calculates the Sun's position from astronomical data rather than assuming a date alone.

| Modality | Cardinal |

| Common themes | Disciplined, Ambitious, Reserved, and Responsible |

## Personality themes

Earth signs are traditionally associated with practicality, patience, tangible results and an appreciation for stability. Their style tends to be grounded and deliberate. Cardinal signs are traditionally described as initiators. They often prefer to begin projects, create movement and respond actively to changing circumstances. Capricorn often approaches life through planning, responsibility and gradual progress toward a durable goal.

Common Capricorn themes include **Disciplined, Ambitious, Reserved, Responsible, Patient**. These are interpretive keywords rather than measurements of a person's personality. A complete natal chart can place the Sun, Moon, rising sign and other planets in different signs, so two people with the same Sun sign can have very different charts.

One useful way to read Capricorn is to combine the three structural layers: element describes the broad style of energy, modality describes how that energy tends to operate, and the ruling planet provides another symbolic lens. This makes the sign more informative than a list of isolated adjectives.

## Strengths and growth areas

Traditional astrology often treats strengths and challenges as two sides of the same pattern. A quality that helps in one situation can become unhelpful when overused.

For Capricorn, **disciplined** energy can support initiative and confidence, while **ambitious** energy can help the sign respond with momentum. At the same time, **reserved** tendencies may need awareness when circumstances require patience or a different strategy.

The goal of a sign interpretation is therefore not to label someone as "good" or "bad." It is to provide a vocabulary for reflecting on patterns, preferences and choices.

## Capricorn in relationships

In traditional compatibility readings, Capricorn is often compared with another person's element, modality, ruling planets and—when birth data is available—the Moon, Venus, Mars and other chart factors.

## How AstroLight calculates a Sun sign

When a visitor uses the Sun-sign calculator, the engine first converts the supplied date and time to a Julian Day and obtains the Sun's apparent geocentric ecliptic longitude from the astronomical ephemeris layer. The tropical zodiac divides 360° into twelve 30° sectors:

`sign index = floor(Sun longitude ÷ 30°)`

For example, a longitude between 120° and 150° belongs to the tropical Leo sector. This is a calculation of astronomical position followed by the conventional zodiac-sector mapping.
