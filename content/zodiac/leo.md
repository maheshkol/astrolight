## Leo at a glance

**Leo (♌)** is traditionally associated with **Fire**, the **Fixed** mode, and **Sun**. The commonly used tropical date range is **July 23 – August 22**. These date ranges are a convenient way to describe Sun-sign astrology; an actual birth chart calculates the Sun's position from astronomical data rather than assuming a date alone.

| Attribute | Traditional association |
|---|---|
| Element | Fire |
| Modality | Fixed |
| Ruling planet | Sun |
| Common dates | July 23 – August 22 |
| Common themes | Confident, Generous, Dramatic, and Loyal |

## Personality themes

Fire signs are traditionally associated with initiative, vitality, confidence and the urge to act. Their style is often direct and expressive. Fixed signs are traditionally described as stabilizers. They often prefer consistency, persistence and enough time to develop an idea or relationship. Leo often approaches life through creative expression, loyalty and the desire to make a visible contribution.

Common Leo themes include **Confident, Generous, Dramatic, Loyal, Stubborn**. These are interpretive keywords rather than measurements of a person's personality. A complete natal chart can place the Sun, Moon, rising sign and other planets in different signs, so two people with the same Sun sign can have very different charts.

One useful way to read Leo is to combine the three structural layers: element describes the broad style of energy, modality describes how that energy tends to operate, and the ruling planet provides another symbolic lens. This makes the sign more informative than a list of isolated adjectives.

## Strengths and growth areas

Traditional astrology often treats strengths and challenges as two sides of the same pattern. A quality that helps in one situation can become unhelpful when overused.

For Leo, **confident** energy can support initiative and confidence, while **generous** energy can help the sign respond with momentum. At the same time, **dramatic** tendencies may need awareness when circumstances require patience or a different strategy.

The goal of a sign interpretation is therefore not to label someone as "good" or "bad." It is to provide a vocabulary for reflecting on patterns, preferences and choices.

## Leo in relationships

In traditional compatibility readings, Leo is often compared with another person's element, modality, ruling planets and—when birth data is available—the Moon, Venus, Mars and other chart factors.

## How AstroLight calculates a Sun sign

When a visitor uses the Sun-sign calculator, the engine first converts the supplied date and time to a Julian Day and obtains the Sun's apparent geocentric ecliptic longitude from the astronomical ephemeris layer. The tropical zodiac divides 360° into twelve 30° sectors:

`sign index = floor(Sun longitude ÷ 30°)`

For example, a longitude between 120° and 150° belongs to the tropical Leo sector. This is a calculation of astronomical position followed by the conventional zodiac-sector mapping.
