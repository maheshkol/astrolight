# Disclaimer

AstroLight provides astrology information, calculations and interactive tools for **educational and entertainment purposes**.

Astrological interpretations describe traditions and symbolic frameworks. They are not scientifically validated methods for diagnosing health conditions, predicting guaranteed outcomes, determining compatibility, or making financial, legal or other professional decisions.

## Calculation accuracy

AstroLight uses astronomical calculations to determine positions such as planetary longitude. Calculation accuracy can still depend on the underlying ephemeris, input date and time, geographic coordinates, time-zone information and the selected astrological convention.

The current public birth-chart calculator uses equal houses and a documented Lahiri-style sidereal approximation. These are choices of the site's calculation model, not universal standards shared by every astrologer.

## Use your judgment

Do not make important medical, financial, legal, employment or relationship decisions solely from an AstroLight result. When professional advice is appropriate, consult a qualified professional.

AstroLight may change its calculations, explanations or tools as the project develops. Where a simplification is used, the site aims to disclose it rather than present it as universal truth.
