### Element dynamic

**Scorpio** is a Water sign and **Pisces** is a Water sign. Water signs are traditionally associated with feeling, intuition, memory and emotional sensitivity. In traditional compatibility readings, the element pairing is used as a broad description of how two styles may meet. It does not determine whether two individuals will get along. People also have Moon signs, rising signs, Venus, Mars and many other chart factors.

### Modality dynamic

Scorpio is **Fixed** while Pisces is **Mutable**. Fixed signs are traditionally described as stabilizers. They often prefer consistency, persistence and enough time to develop an idea or relationship. Mutable signs are traditionally described as adapters. They often respond well to changing information, different viewpoints and evolving circumstances.

This can create a useful contrast: one person may want to initiate while the other wants to consolidate, or one may prefer a stable plan while the other adapts as new information appears.

### Relationship themes

A useful reading of this pairing starts with what each sign values. **Scorpio** is commonly associated with passionate, resourceful, secretive, while **Pisces** is commonly associated with compassionate, artistic, escapist. These keywords can describe complementary strengths as well as potential friction.

Rather than treating the score as a verdict, use the comparison as a set of questions:

- Where do the two signs naturally understand each other?
- Where could their different pacing create tension?
- Which strengths can compensate for the other's blind spots?
- What additional birth-chart information would make the comparison more specific?

### How AstroLight calculates the score

The model is deterministic:

`score = normalize(element points + modality points + sign-wheel points)`

The sign-wheel component uses the shortest distance between the two signs on the 12-sign circle. This is a deliberately simple educational model. It is not a scientific compatibility test and does not claim to predict relationship outcomes.

For a deeper comparison, use two complete birth charts and compare the Moon, Venus, Mars, Ascendants and planetary aspects rather than relying on Sun signs alone.
