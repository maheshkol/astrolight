# About AstroLight

AstroLight is an educational astrology website built around a simple idea: **show the calculation first, then explain the traditional interpretation**.

The site combines astronomical position data, mathematical coordinate transformations and astrology reference material. Its interactive tools are designed to make concepts such as zodiac degrees, planetary positions, houses and Nakshatras easier to explore.

## What AstroLight calculates

Depending on the tool, AstroLight can calculate:

- tropical zodiac sign placement;
- planetary ecliptic longitudes;
- Julian Day;
- local sidereal time;
- the Ascendant;
- equal-house cusps and house placement;
- sidereal positions using a Lahiri-style ayanamsa approximation;
- Nakshatra and pada from sidereal longitude;
- angular relationships between planets;
- an explicitly disclosed Sun-sign compatibility score.

Planetary positions are obtained from Astronomy Engine, an open-source astronomical calculation library. The rest of the site's calculations are documented in the AstroLight mathematics section.

## Calculation versus interpretation

A chart position is a calculated quantity. Its astrological meaning is an interpretation from a traditional system. AstroLight keeps these layers separate so that visitors can see what the software calculated and what is being interpreted.

Astrology is presented for educational and entertainment purposes. AstroLight does not claim that astrology scientifically predicts personality, health, financial outcomes, relationships or future events.

## Why the site is being built this way

Many astrology websites present a result without explaining how it was produced. AstroLight aims to be more transparent: users should be able to follow a result from birth data to degrees, from degrees to signs or houses, and from those coordinates to an interpretive explanation.

The project is continuously being expanded with new calculators, visualizations and educational articles.
