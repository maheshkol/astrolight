How the score is calculated

AstroLight uses a transparent Sun-sign compatibility model based on three disclosed components:

  • Element — 18 points maximum: compares the elemental relationship between the two signs.
  • Modality — 14 points maximum: compares Cardinal, Fixed and Mutable qualities.
  • Sign-wheel distance — 20 points maximum: uses the shortest distance between the two signs around the zodiac wheel.

The three components produce a maximum raw score of 52 points. AstroLight scales that result to a percentage for easier reading.

This is an educational reflection model, not a scientific measure or prediction of relationship success.
