SEE THE MATH

Numbers first. Meaning second.

A zodiac sign occupies 30° of the 360° ecliptic. The 27 Nakshatras divide that same circle into 13°20′ sections, each split into four 3°20′ padas.

360° ÷ 12 = 30°360° ÷ 27 = 13°20′
