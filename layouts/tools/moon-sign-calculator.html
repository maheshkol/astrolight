How it is calculated

01

Geocentric Moon

AstroLight calculates the Moon's geocentric ecliptic longitude for the supplied birth moment.

02

Sidereal conversion

The tropical longitude is converted to a sidereal longitude using a Lahiri-style ayanamsa.

03

Moon sign

The sidereal longitude is divided into twelve 30° zodiac signs to determine the Vedic Moon sign.

04

Nakshatra & Pada

The sidereal longitude is divided across 27 Nakshatras, each spanning 13°20′, with four Padas of 3°20′ each.

Moon sign, Nakshatra and Pada

Moon sign (Rashi)

The sidereal sign occupied by the Moon at birth. In Jyotisha, the Moon is traditionally associated with the mind, emotions, habits and subjective experience.

Nakshatra

The lunar mansion occupied by the Moon. Each Nakshatra covers 13°20′ of the sidereal zodiac and has four Padas.

Pada

The quarter of the Nakshatra containing the Moon. Each Pada spans 3°20′ and adds a more specific traditional layer of interpretation.

AstroLight presents the astronomical calculation separately from traditional Jyotisha symbolism. Astrological interpretations are offered for cultural and reflective purposes and are not scientific predictions or guarantees.
